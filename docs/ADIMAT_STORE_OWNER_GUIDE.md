# Adimat POS
Store Owner Guide
Role-specific guide for owners. The in-app Learn Adimat area shows this guidance only to a signed-in store owner.

## Open and set up your store

Open Adimat on a supported phone, tablet, or computer. Use the Store ID and owner PIN supplied by your administrator, then sign in with your PIN. Install the app from the browser menu if you want it on your home screen. Open it once online before relying on the offline shell.

Complete the store profile in Settings. Add each staff member with a separate PIN. Choose staff permissions, discount limits, and the automatic idle timeout. Do not share the owner PIN.

## Use your owner dashboard

Home summarizes today's completed sales, cash and MoMo/Paystack totals, and products needing stock attention. The quick actions open a new sale, add a product, or record an expense. Figures show data currently available on this device; sync when you use more than one device.

## Manage products and stock

Open Inventory to add or edit a product's name, category, prices, barcode, photo, and stock. Use CSV import for a product list and check the import results. Set a Restock alert quantity for products you want in reorder suggestions. Use Restock & audit to review recorded receipts, transfers, sales, and adjustments.

Use Purchasing to save suppliers and create purchase orders. When a delivery arrives, receive the full order or actual partial quantity into the correct shop or warehouse. Receiving updates stock and product cost. Cancel only an order that is still open.

If Warehouse is enabled, select the correct location before stock receipt, transfer, expiry review, or adjustment. Reports can be exported for individual locations. Products may support expiry tracking, pack sizes, and wholesale prices. At checkout, use the intended sale price; expiry batches are deducted first-expiring-first-out.

## Expenses and reports

Record expenses in Expenses; attach a receipt photo when available. Set monthly category budgets and recurring monthly expenses for predictable costs. Recurring entries are created when the app is active or the Expenses screen opens.

Reports can be filtered to today, this week, or this month. Review sales by payment method, products, expenses, cashier activity, and estimated net profit. Export supported reports to CSV or PDF. Use Cash Book to monitor cash movements and manage cashier access in Settings → Staff → Perms.

## Online shop and subscription

If the administrator enables the public storefront, sync the store and open Online to copy the share link, list products, and review pay-on-delivery orders. Give cashiers order-management permission only when needed.

Use Plan to view subscription status and administrator contact information. Set the store's preferred Mobile Money payout account in Settings → Mobile Money Payout, then sync the store.

## Sync, backup, and security

Cash sales can be recorded offline after the app has loaded; Paystack payments and cloud sync need internet. Settings → Cloud Sync provides Full Sync, Pull, and Push. Check last sync, pending changes, and errors.

Use Settings → Backup & sync health to download a store backup or restore one on the current device. Daily automatic snapshots stay on the device and are not protection against device loss. Store downloaded backups somewhere separate and secure; they contain sensitive staff data. Restore replaces local data for the selected store.

## Owner checklist

- Check the correct store and location before changing stock.
- Use unique staff PINs and review permissions when roles change.
- Sync before using another device and after restoring a backup.
- Keep a separate secure backup and review expenses and sales regularly.
- Ask the administrator for help enabling optional warehouse or online-shop features.
