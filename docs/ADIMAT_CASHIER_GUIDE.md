# Adimat POS
Cashier Guide
Role-specific guide for cashiers. The in-app Learn Adimat area shows cashier lessons only and adds optional topics only when the store owner has enabled that access.

## Sign in safely

Open Adimat and sign in with your own cashier PIN. Never use another staff member's PIN or share yours. The owner manages cashier accounts and can reset a PIN. If the till has been idle, sign in again; the cart may have been cleared by the idle lock.

## Make a sale

1. Open POS. Search for a product by name or barcode, scan it with the camera, or use a paired handheld scanner.
2. Tap the product to add it. Check the cart items, quantities, and total before checkout.
3. Choose Cash, Paystack, or Split and follow the payment instructions. Paystack needs internet.
4. Wait for the payment result. Do not start the same checkout again while it is processing.
5. After confirmation, provide the customer with the receipt: PDF, share, WhatsApp where supported, or a connected Bluetooth printer.

Adimat checks stock during a sale. If an item is out of stock or the quantity is unavailable, do not adjust stock yourself; tell the owner. If a discount needs owner authorization, ask the owner to enter their PIN.

## Find sales and hand over your shift

Open My Sales to review your transactions and receipts. At shift end, follow the store's cash-count and handover procedure, and report any mismatch to the owner. Use Switch user so the next staff member can sign in with their own PIN.

## Optional tools your owner enabled

Cash Book appears only if your owner enabled access for your account. Use it according to the store's cash recording rules.

Online order management appears only when the store has an enabled storefront and your owner granted you permission. Review the Online inbox and update order fulfilment as instructed by your store.

Devices may show Cloud Sync controls. Use them only as instructed by your owner; check My Sales after syncing if a sale needs confirmation. Do not clear browser storage to fix a sync problem, because it may erase work on this device.

## If something goes wrong

- If a payment is still processing, wait and check My Sales before trying again.
- If a barcode scan fails, search by product name or type the barcode.
- If the camera or printer does not appear, check browser permission and ask the owner for help.
- If a product's stock is wrong, pause the sale and contact the owner.
- Never send your PIN, customer information, or store backup in a support message.
